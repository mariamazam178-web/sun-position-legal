1. What the app provides

Sun Position is an informational tool that calculates and displays:

  • Sun and moon rise/set times, twilight phases and positions
  • Milky Way visibility windows and galactic-center direction
  • An AR-style direction overlay over the camera preview

2. Informational purpose only

All values shown by the app are approximations produced by astronomical calculations (using the open-source astronomy-engine library). The AR overlay additionally relies on device sensors and a fixed assumed camera field of view.
