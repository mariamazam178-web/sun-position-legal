Privacy Policy

Sun Position · Last updated: February 2026

The short version: Sun Position collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising SDKs. Everything the app knows about you stays on your device.

1. Location

  • The app uses your device's location only to calculate sun, moon and Milky Way positions for where you are.
  • All calculations run on your device. Your coordinates are never sent to us or to any third party by this app.
  • You can deny the location permission and still use the app with saved locations or the map.

2. Saved locations

Locations you save in the Planner are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded, synced or shared. Deleting the app deletes them.

3. Camera (AR view)

The AR view uses your camera only to show the direction overlay on top of the live image. Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded. The camera frames never leave your device.

4. Map

The map screen displays map tiles from OpenStreetMap contributors. When you view the map, your device connects to OpenStreetMap's tile servers and your IP address is processed by them according to the OpenStreetMap privacy policy. This app adds no tracking to those requests.

5. Data we never touch

  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone — the app does not request access.
  • Identifiers, device IDs, usage statistics — not collected.
  • Cookies — none.

6. Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, so it complies with COPPA and similar regulations by design.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships. The current version always lives here and in the app's Settings screen.

8. Contact

The app is open source. Questions or concerns can be raised in the project's GitHub repository.
